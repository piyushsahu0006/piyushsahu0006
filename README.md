## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **BBA student associated with IBM**, exploring the intersection of **Business, Data, Artificial Intelligence and Technology**.

But my interest in business didn't start in a classroom.

I've been exposed to my family's **bath-fitting business since around Class 7**. Over the years, I've seen the practical side of business — from products and suppliers to customers, inventory, pricing and everyday decision-making.

That experience made me curious about something:

> **What if real business decisions could be understood better through data and technology?**

That's the direction I'm exploring today.

I'm learning **Python, SQL, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, AI and LLM-based tools**, while building projects that help me understand how technology can solve practical problems.

I'm not trying to learn everything at once.

I'm focused on one thing:

**Learn → Build → Analyse → Improve**
